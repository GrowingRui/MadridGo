## In short
MadridGo has no accounts and does not track you. We do not use advertising or analytics. Your
saved places and settings stay on your phone.

## What the app uses
- **Your location**, only if you allow it and tap "Current location" (or, once you have allowed it,
  when you open Departures). It is sent with that request to find nearby stops or plan a journey.
  We do not store it and do not link it to you. (Requires the access-log fix in SECURITY_REVIEW.md finding 1 before this sentence is true.)
- **What you type in search** (stop names, addresses), sent to our server to return results.
  Address searches are passed on to CartoCiudad (Instituto Geográfico Nacional); they see our
  server, not you.
- **Saved on your device only:** starred stops, saved journeys, recent searches, language, dark
  mode, accessibility settings and offline copies of boards and routes. Deleting the app deletes
  them. "Clear" buttons in the app remove them earlier.

## Third parties that see your connection
- **OpenFreeMap** supplies map tiles when you open a map; it can see your IP address.
- Transport data comes from Renfe, EMT Madrid and the Consorcio Regional de Transportes de Madrid
  (CRTM); the app shows their attribution. They receive requests from our server, not from you.

## What we keep on the server
Server logs (time, page requested without search details or coordinates, status) kept for at most [TO FILL, suggested 14] days to
keep the service running and detect abuse. Timetable and punctuality statistics, which contain no
personal data.

## Your rights (GDPR)
Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, there is normally nothing to access,
correct or delete. If you think we do, write to [TO FILL] and we will answer within one month. You
may complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

## Changes
We will change the "Last updated" date and say what changed in the app's About page.
